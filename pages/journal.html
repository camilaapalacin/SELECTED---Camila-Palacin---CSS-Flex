01 / CULTURE — 08.09.26 — 8 MIN READ

THE ART OF
SELECTION.

Why choosing what to listen to matters in a world where everything is available.

In a culture of endless access, attention becomes the rarest resource. Selection is how we give it direction.

MAKING SPACE FOR DISCOVERY

There has never been more music within reach. Every platform promises more tracks, more playlists and more recommendations. But abundance does not always lead to discovery. Sometimes it makes it harder to stop, listen and connect.

Curation offers another way through the noise. It is a personal gesture: a record passed from one listener to another, a mix that tells a story, a space where a sound can be experienced without rushing to the next thing.

THE VALUE OF A POINT OF VIEW

To select is not to close a door. It is to create a path. A thoughtful selection can introduce unfamiliar voices, connect distant scenes and make unexpected relationships visible. The archive begins with that simple intention: leave a trace of what moves us.

LESS NOISE. MORE MEANING. ALWAYS CURATED.

02 / PEOPLE — 28.08.26 — 6 MIN READ

MIRA K.: SOUND,
INTUITION & COMMUNITY.

A conversation around creative process, listening and shared experiences.

A DJ set is never only a sequence of tracks. It is also a reading of a room, a response to a moment and an exchange with the people listening.

LISTENING AS A PRACTICE

This editorial preview follows the ideas behind an independent music practice: collecting references, building connections between sounds and allowing a selection to evolve naturally.

From the first record to the final transition, the process is shaped by curiosity. Genres can offer useful coordinates, but the most interesting moments often happen between them.

COMMUNITY IN THE ROOM

The dancefloor is a shared space. Each person brings a different history, and every gathering creates a temporary community around sound. That exchange is part of what makes a session worth documenting.

03 / PLACES — 14.08.26 — 7 MIN READ

BERLIN STILL
SOUNDS DIFFERENT.

A journey through a city that continues to inspire new ways of listening.

A city is not a genre. It is a collection of rooms, routines, memories and people that shape how music is made and heard.

THE SPACES BETWEEN

Studios, basements, record shops and temporary venues form a network of places where ideas travel. Their character comes from the people who use them and the scenes that gather around them.

Looking at a city through sound means looking beyond its best-known addresses. It means paying attention to small encounters, independent projects and the spaces that make experimentation possible.

04 / IDEAS — 02.08.26 — 5 MIN READ

MUSIC IS TEMPORARY.
THE ARCHIVE REMAINS.

On documenting the present and keeping its cultural signals alive.

A night ends, a mix fades and a space changes. Documentation gives a moment another life without pretending it can be repeated.

KEEPING A TRACE

An archive is never neutral or complete. It is a living collection shaped by the decisions of the people who build it. What gets included, how it is described and which voices are heard all matter.

SELECTED approaches the archive as an ongoing conversation: a way to connect sessions, people, places and ideas across time.

TOOLS OF
SELECTION.

Devices, formats and objects that shape the way we experience music.

The tools we use affect the way we listen. A record sleeve, a mixer, a pair of headphones or a notebook can become part of a personal ritual.

FORMAT, FEELING, MEMORY

Objects carry practical functions, but they also hold memories. They connect a listener to a place, a period or a particular way of approaching sound.

This collection is an open notebook of the objects, formats and instruments that support the culture around listening.
