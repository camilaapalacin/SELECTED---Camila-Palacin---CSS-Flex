01 / MANIFESTOTHE ART OF SELECTION

OUR
MANIFESTO+

We believe that what deserves attention is worth discovering.

SELECTED is an independent platform dedicated to underground electronic culture. We discover and document artists, sounds, spaces and ideas that deserve to be heard.

In a world where music is everywhere, finding something meaningful can be harder than ever. We put human curation back at the centre of discovery.
